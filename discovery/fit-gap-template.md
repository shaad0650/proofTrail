## Rule for a material gap

A gap is material only if the participant demonstrates that:

- the current tool does not already provide acceptable functionality;
- the remaining manual step is recurring;
- staff time or rework is meaningful;
- buyer-independent supplier-side coordination is not already solved; and
- the limitation is not dominated by unrelated buyer liquidity or contract terms.

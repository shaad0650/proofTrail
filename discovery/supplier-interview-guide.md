# Supplier Interview Guide

## Rules

- Ask about the last specific invoice, not a hypothetical future product.
- Do not lead with ProofTrail or ask whether the participant "would use" it.
- Do not collect GSTIN, bank details, confidential invoices, or unredacted
  customer data during discovery.
- Obtain explicit permission before recording or retaining a sample.
- Separate supplier-reported causes from buyer-confirmed causes.

## Context

1. Describe the business and the buyers served.
2. How many B2B invoices are issued per month?
3. How many require special documents or buyer-specific handling?
4. Who prepares, submits, tracks, and resolves each invoice?

## Walk through the last invoice

1. What triggered the invoice?
2. Which PO, work order, delivery, quality, and acceptance records existed?
3. Who determined the buyer's requirements?
4. What tools or locations held each source record?
5. What documents were prepared, and in what order?
6. How was the invoice submitted?
7. What evidence was retained for submission?
8. How was acknowledgment recorded?
9. Was the invoice accepted, rejected, queried, disputed, or paid?
10. What follow-up was required and who owned it?

## Current-tool gap

1. Which accounting software, portal, email, WhatsApp, spreadsheet, or paper
   process is used?
2. What does each tool store already?
3. What manual step remains after those tools are used?
4. When did the process fail or require rework?
5. What was the frequency of that failure?
6. How much staff time was spent?
7. What cost, delay, or relationship impact was observed?
8. Has a buyer AP or procurement contact confirmed the cause?

## Evidence request

Ask for, only with consent:

- an anonymized status timeline;
- a redacted checklist or instruction;
- a redacted resubmission or rejection example;
- proof of existing-tool limitation.

Do not accept or retain:

- passwords or credentials;
- unredacted identity or tax information;
- bank details;
- confidential contractual terms;
- employer or customer systems data.

## Close

1. Summarize the workflow in the participant's own terms.
2. Ask for corrections to frequency, cost, or sequence.
3. Record whether the participant will join a supervised concierge pilot.
4. Do not make any payment or purchase commitment for the participant.

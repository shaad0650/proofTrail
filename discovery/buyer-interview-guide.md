# Buyer AP/Procurement Interview Guide

## Rules

- Ask about a recently observed invoice, while preserving supplier anonymity.
- Confirm causes before attributing rejection or delay to missing evidence.
- Do not ask for confidential portal data or supplier/customer documents.
- Do not request credentials or promise a portal integration.

## Interview questions

1. What information is required before an invoice can enter the buyer workflow?
2. What references are mandatory versus contextual?
3. How are PO, invoice, delivery, GRN, and quality evidence matched?
4. Which records are manual versus system-confirmed?
5. What does submission acknowledgment mean in your process?
6. What is the difference between receipt, acknowledgment, acceptance, and payment?
7. Which rejection reasons are common?
8. Which rejections occur before reconciliation versus after quality/contract review?
9. Which statuses are visible to suppliers?
10. How is supporting evidence delivered and retained?
11. Which manual coordination remains after a supplier submits?
12. Where does existing accounting or buyer-portal functionality already solve the gap?

## Validation record

For each reported supplier problem, classify:

- **buyer-confirmed**: buyer described the same cause;
- **supplier-only**: only supplier reported it;
- **unverified**: no source confirmed it;
- **not applicable**: buyer process does not produce that issue.

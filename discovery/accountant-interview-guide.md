# CA/Accountant Interview Guide

## Rules

- Ask about recurring patterns across anonymized client cases.
- Do not collect client identity, GST, bank, or confidential invoice data.
- Do not assume the accountant is the economic buyer.

## Interview questions

1. Which documentation problems recur across supplier clients?
2. Where are invoices held after submission?
3. How are rejections and acceptance/acknowledgment evidence tracked?
4. How much time is spent locating or assembling evidence?
5. Which tools or spreadsheets are currently used?
6. What existing functionality remains unavailable or disconnected?
7. Would a supplier or its owner be the likely payer?
8. What controls or confidentiality requirements would apply?
9. What evidence would justify recommending or deploying a workflow tool?
10. What would make a paid pilot fail or be rejected?
